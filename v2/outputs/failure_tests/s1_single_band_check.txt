Sentinel-1 one-band processing check (2026-10-28)

Harvest year 2015 is the only Sentinel-1 year processed as a one-band image
(flash flood only; no complete Jun-Oct 2014 monsoon season). To confirm that
it follows the same rules as later years, harvest year 2016 was recomputed
through the same one-band path and compared with the stored 2016 values,
which were computed through the usual two-band path.

Districts compared: 64
Maximum absolute difference: 9.97e-17 (floating-point noise)
Result: identical
